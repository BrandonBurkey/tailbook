# Partnerships: Podcasts, YouTubers, Aviation Media, Outreach Templates

**Already covered elsewhere (don't duplicate):** type-club outreach (COPA, ABS, CPA, POS, MAPA) and the General Aviation News article pitch are in `validation/04-emails.md` §7–8. The guest article itself is `validation/05-guest-article.md`. This file covers **podcasts, YouTube creators, the wider aviation media, and A&P/IA shops**, plus the templates for them.

**Verification:** every outlet and creator below was confirmed to exist by web search on **2026-10-19** (sources linked). Audience sizes come from third-party trackers and are **approximate and possibly stale**; check the channel or the media kit before quoting a number to anyone. Contact details aren't listed on purpose: find the current one on each show's or creator's own site the day you reach out.

**Rules for every partnership** (from `brand-voice.md` §0):
- Disclose. The founder is building TailBook, isn't an A&P, and an IA reviews the rules.
- Creators must disclose any material connection (a free subscription, a code, payment) per the **FTC Endorsement Guides**: a spoken "TailBook gave me…" plus the platform's paid-partnership label. Put this in writing.
- Never ask a creator to say TailBook makes an airplane airworthy or legal. Give them the approved talking points (§5).
- **App Store guideline 5.6:** never trade perks for *ratings or reviews*. A creator can review the app on their channel; they can't be paid to leave App Store reviews.

## 5. Approved talking points for any partner

1. TailBook shows what's due on your airplane (annual, transponder, pitot-static, ELT inspection and battery, registration, VOR check, oil, and the recurring ADs *you* enter), each with its FAR citation.
2. It does the calendar-month math (end of the month) and warns you at 60, 30 and 7 days.
3. Setup is about eight dates plus your tach; there's no logbook digitizing to start and no account. Data stays on your device and your iCloud.
4. Co-owners share one board. Pricing is per aircraft: $199/yr; Founding Owners $149/yr for life for the first 250 aircraft.
5. **It doesn't decide airworthiness or which ADs apply. Your A&P/IA and the pilot in command do.**
6. The founder isn't an A&P; an IA reviews the rules engine.

**Never say:** "keeps you legal", "makes sure you're airworthy", "FAA-approved", "replaces your mechanic".

## 6. A&P/IA shops (the referral channel from research §4.1)

**When:** after the app ships (shops need something to see). Pre-launch, only the IA advisor relationship.
**Offer:** 20 independent shops get counter cards plus a free way to view shared Status Reports (the owner exports a PDF; no shop account is needed in v1).
**Counter card copy (A6):**
> **Bring your due dates to your annual.**
> Show us your TailBook Status Report at drop-off and we'll plan your 24-month checks in the same visit.
> [QR → `[CHECKER_URL]?utm_source=shop-{shopname}&utm_medium=qr&utm_campaign=launch-{yyyymm}`]
> *TailBook organizes the dates you enter. Your A&P/IA determines airworthiness.*

Ask the shop's permission for the exact wording; never print a shop's name or logo without written OK.

## 7. Outreach templates

### 7a. Creator outreach (YouTube/Instagram), first message
Short, specific, and no ask to post. Send by email if listed; otherwise a DM.

> **Subject:** Your [specific video] + a question from an owner-app builder
>
> Hi [first name],
>
> I watched your [video title], especially [one specific moment, e.g. "the part where you dug through the C35's logbooks"]. I'm [Founder name], and I'm building **TailBook**, an iPhone app that shows owners what's due on their airplane, with the regulation behind each date. Full disclosure: I'm not an A&P, and an IA reviews the rules.
>
> I'm not asking you to post anything. I'd value **15 minutes of your honest reaction** to the board as an owner. You'd see it before anyone else, and if you think it's wrong-headed, that's exactly what I need to hear.
>
> If it turns out useful, your [aircraft] gets a free Founding Owner year, no strings, no posting required.
>
> Free checker if you want a look first: [CHECKER_URL]?utm_source=yt-{creator}&utm_medium=video&utm_campaign=creator-outreach
>
> Thanks either way, and fly safe.
> [Founder name] · founder@[domain]

**Follow-up (once, 7 days later):**
> Hi [first name], one nudge on this. Happy to send a 60-second screen recording instead of a call if that's easier. No reply needed if it's not for you.

### 7b. Creator launch offer (only after the 15-minute feedback, or if they reached out)
> Hi [first name], TailBook is live on the App Store. If you'd like to show it, here's what I can offer:
> - Your [aircraft] free for [1 year / as long as you use it]. That's a material connection, so please mention it on-screen ("TailBook gave me a free subscription") and use the platform's paid-partnership label. FTC rules, and it's how I'd want it anyway.
> - A custom App Store offer code for your audience: **[CODE]** for [first year at $149 (Founding price) / X% off]. *(Verify the exact discount structure in App Store Connect first.)*
> - [Optional, if budget allows: $[X] flat for a dedicated segment, or $[Y] per paid annual subscription redeemed with your code, paid monthly from App Store Connect's offer-code redemption report.]
>
> Talking points are attached (one page). Two hard lines: please don't say TailBook makes an airplane airworthy or legal (your IA and the PIC decide that), and please don't ask viewers for App Store ratings in exchange for anything. Everything else is your honest opinion, including what you don't like.

### 7c. Podcast guest pitch
> **Subject:** Guest idea for [show]: "The items owners forget aren't the annual"
>
> Hi [host first name],
>
> Long-time listener of [show]; your episode on [specific episode] [one-line genuine reaction].
>
> Segment idea for your audience: **the long-interval items owners forget**: the 24-month transponder and pitot-static checks (91.413/91.411), the ELT's two separate clocks (91.207), the registration that now runs seven years (47.40), and the "calendar month" argument that never dies on Pilots of America. 15–25 minutes, practical, regulation-cited, with a free one-page sheet for your show notes.
>
> Who I am, honestly: I'm building TailBook, an owner-side due-date app, and I'm not an A&P. An IA reviews the material, and I can bring them along if you'd prefer a mechanic's voice. I'll keep the product mention to one line at the end, or none if that's your rule.
>
> [Founder name] · founder@[domain] · [phone]

## 1. How this fits the validation playbook (and what it doesn't duplicate)

The playbook already runs the **forum posts (E1)**, the **email sequences**, **interviews** and the **guest article**. This calendar adds **owned social channels** that:
1. **Feed the same funnel:** video/post → free checker (`checker_completed`) → email or Snapshot (`email_submitted`, `snapshot_clicked`) → Founding Owner deposit (`deposit_clicked`).
2. **Give forum posts a credibility trail.** When a POA regular clicks your profile, they find 15 accurate, cited explainers instead of an empty account.
3. **Build a launch audience** for the App Store release (`launch-plan.md`).

**Attribution is kept separate from E1.** E1 counts only `utm_campaign=e1` (forum medium). Social links use `utm_medium=social` and `utm_campaign=organic-w{1-8}`, so the E1 readout on **Nov 15** stays clean. Snapshot requests and deposits from social are logged in the tracker's `channel_breakdown` as `social:{platform}`.

**Facebook groups:** the playbook's E1 posts (Variant A image post / Variant B poll) happen on W1's weekend and in W3. This calendar **doesn't repost those**. FB rows here are (a) sharing a short video in a group whose admin already approved TailBook content, or (b) value replies in existing threads, no link unless asked.

**Capacity guardrail:** Snapshot delivery is capped at 8/day (SOP). If social pushes requests above ~5/day for 3 days, switch the CTA on new posts from "free Snapshot" to "free checker" (self-serve) until the queue clears.

**Time budget:** ~4 hours/week on top of the playbook. Batch-film on **Sunday** (2 videos, about 90 minutes), post on schedule, reply to comments for 15 minutes a day.

## 5. The 15 scripts

**Production notes for every script**
- **Length:** 30–45 seconds. **Hook in the first 2 seconds**, both spoken and as on-screen text. No logo intro, no "Hey guys".
- **Shot list:** founder to camera (phone on a tripod, window light), plus B-roll: logbook pages (the founder's practice aircraft or a friend's, **with permission and N-number blurred**), sticker/label close-ups, and phone screen recordings of the checker.
- **Captions burned in** (most people watch muted). Citations appear on screen in monospace.
- **End card (last 3 s, every video):** "Free due-date checker: link in bio" + the short disclaimer.
- **Before posting:** run the hook test in `brand-voice.md` §5, and re-check each citation against eCFR.
- **Caption template:** 1-line restatement of the hook · 2–3 lines of the fact · "Free checker (no signup to see results): link in bio" · "Disclosure: I'm building TailBook. TailBook organizes the dates you enter; your A&P/IA and PIC determine airworthiness." · 3–5 hashtags (#aircraftowner #generalaviation #avgeek #pilotlife #[type]owner).

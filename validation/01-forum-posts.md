## Universal etiquette (read before every post)
1. **Read the forum's current rules and pinned posts first.** Rules change, and the notes below are guidance, not gospel. **When in doubt, message a moderator before posting**: "I'm building an owner tool; I'd like to share a free cheat sheet. Is that OK, and where?" Mods almost always say yes to an honest ask, and they remove surprises.
2. **Earn standing first.** Make 5–10 genuinely useful replies before your first link post. Many forums restrict links for new accounts.
3. **One post per forum per variant.** Never cross-post the same text on the same day. Never bump your own thread.
4. **Answer every reply within 4 hours,** including the critical ones. "Why not just use Excel?" is a gift. Answer it honestly: "Excel works great if you do the month-end math right; the sheet shows how."
5. **Never claim to determine airworthiness.** If asked about a specific situation: "That's one for your IA. I'd hate to guess wrong on your airplane."
6. **Put the attachment directly in the post where you can** (the PDF, or the content inline). A post that's useful without clicking builds trust and gets fewer mod removals.
7. **Don't argue with the regulars.** If a veteran A&P corrects you, thank them publicly and fix the sheet. That's free IA review, and readers notice.
8. **Log everything in the tracker the same day:** post URL, date, views if visible, replies, clicks (Plausible), requests (Tally).

## A/B testing protocol
- **A = value/template-led; B = question/conversation-led.** Run A first in each channel, then B 7+ days later, ideally replying in an existing thread rather than starting a new one.
- **The winning metric is Snapshot requests per post**, not clicks or likes. Second metric: requests per 100 clicks.
- With these small numbers you won't get statistical significance. Treat a variant as winning only if it produces **2× the requests** across at least 3 channels. Otherwise call it a tie and keep whichever is easier to post.
- Log replies by type: question / correction / "I use Excel" / "where do I sign up" / hostile. The "where do I sign up" count is the gold.

# Customer Interviews (Mom Test style)

**Goal:** 15–20 owner conversations by Dec 13 (about 3–5 a week). Learn how owners **actually** track due items today, what it has cost them, and whether they have ever **paid** to fix it.
**Rule of the Mom Test:** ask about specifics in the past, not opinions about the future. Compliments, "I'd use that" and "great idea" are worth **zero**. Talk 20% of the time and listen 80%.

---

## 1. Recruiting

**Where:** every Snapshot requester (the warmest), forum replies ("you said you use a whiteboard, could I ask you 3 questions?"), your local FBO/EAA chapter/flying club, type-club members, and hangar neighbors on a Saturday.

**Forum DM / email (cold)**
> Subject: 15 minutes on how you track your [Bonanza]'s due dates?
>
> Hi [Name], I saw your post about [tracking with a spreadsheet / your annual]. I'm researching how owners keep track of inspections and records. (Disclosure: I'm building an app for this, but this is not a sales call. I won't pitch you anything.)
>
> Would you give me 15 minutes by phone this week? I'm trying to learn what's actually annoying, not what sounds good. As a thank-you, I'll build you a free Airworthiness Snapshot from photos of your recent entries, or send a $25 Amazon card, your choice.
>
> [Calendar link] · [Your name] · founder@[domain]

**Snapshot requester (warm)**
> Your Snapshot is on its way within 48h. While I'm building it, could I ask you 15 minutes of questions about how you track things now? It'll help me get yours right. [Calendar link]

**In person (hangar / FBO)**
> "Nice [airplane]. How long have you had her? ... I'm working on a project about how owners keep track of inspections and records. Mind if I ask how you do it?"

**Incentive:** a free Snapshot (best: it also feeds E2), or a $25 gift card (budget ~$250). Never offer a discount on the future app as the incentive, because it biases answers.

## 2. The script (15–25 minutes)

**Open (1 min)**
> "Thanks. I'm not selling anything today. I'm trying to learn how owners handle maintenance tracking. There are no right answers, and 'I don't really bother' is a great answer. Mind if I take notes?"

**Context (3 min)**
1. "What do you fly, and how long have you owned it?"
2. "Solo owner or partnership? How many partners?"
3. "Roughly how many hours a year? Do you fly IFR?"
4. "Who does your annual: your own IA, or a shop?"

**Current behavior: the core (8–10 min).** Dig into specifics every time with "Tell me about the last time…"
5. "Walk me through how you know when something's coming due. **Show me** if you can." (Screen-share their spreadsheet or whiteboard if they're willing. This is gold.)
6. "When did you last update that? What triggered it?"
7. "**Tell me about the last time something snuck up on you,** an inspection, an AD, a registration. What happened? What did it cost: money, a missed trip, a grounded weekend?"
   - If "never": "What's the closest call?" If still never, note it and move on. Don't lead.
8. "When your last annual came around, what did you do to prepare? How long did it take?"
9. "Where are your logbooks right now? Have you ever scanned or copied them? Why or why not?"
10. "When you bought the airplane, how did the records factor in? Did anything in them affect the price?"
11. (Partnerships) "How do partners log tach time? Tell me about the last time that went wrong."

**Money and alternatives (3–5 min)**
12. "Have you ever paid for anything to help with this, like a service, an app, or your shop tracking it? What happened with it? Why did you stop or keep it?"
13. "Have you looked for a tool? What did you try? What did you search for?"
14. "What does your insurer ask you for at renewal?"

**Close (2 min)**
15. "Is there anything I should have asked but didn't?"
16. "Who else should I talk to? Could you introduce me?" (Count intros. Real pain produces intros.)
17. **Only at the end, and only if they've shown pain:** "I'm offering a free Snapshot and a founding-owner deposit for the app I'm building. Want me to send the details?" Then **stop talking.** The answer, and especially whether they follow through, is your strongest signal.

**Never ask:** "Would you use an app that…?", "How much would you pay?", "Do you think this is a good idea?", or "Wouldn't it be great if…?"

**If they ask you an airworthiness question:** "I'm not qualified to answer that. Your IA is the right person."

## 4. Validation vs polite noise

**Counts as validation (strongest first)**
1. **Money:** places the $49 deposit. Nothing else comes close.
2. **Time and data:** sends real logbook photos for a Snapshot (effort plus privacy given up).
3. **Reputation:** introduces you to another owner, their partner, their IA, or their club.
4. **Built their own workaround:** a spreadsheet with formulas, a whiteboard, a laminated card. This proves the job exists.
5. **Already pays for something similar** (SavvyMx, a shop tracking fee, Crewchief), or stopped paying and can say exactly why.
6. **A specific, costly past incident** with a date and a dollar or trip cost ("Lapsed transponder check in March 2025, missed a trip to Oshkosh").
7. **Searched for a tool** and can name what they tried.

**Polite noise (do not count)**
- "That's a great idea!" / "I'd totally use that." / "You should build it."
- "I'd pay $X for that" (a hypothetical price).
- "Let me know when it's out" with no Snapshot or deposit.
- "My buddy would love this" with no actual intro.
- Feature requests without a story behind them.
- Enthusiasm from non-owners (renters, students, enthusiasts). Tag them `not-a-fit`.

**Negative signals (record them as carefully as positives)**
- "My shop tracks all that, I just show up." Who are these owners? If they're common, consider the A&P/IA shop segment pivot.
- "I've never had anything sneak up on me" from a meticulous spreadsheet user, which means the E3 aha won't happen for them.
- "I only care about not losing the logbooks," a signal for the Logbook Vault pivot (`06-decision-rules.md`).
- "I'd never put my logbooks in an app" (privacy). Count them.

**Weekly synthesis (every Friday, 20 min):** tally the signal tags, write the top 3 quotes into the tracker notes, and answer one line: *"What did I believe on Monday that I no longer believe?"*

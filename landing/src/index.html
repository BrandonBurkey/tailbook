      Why owners get caught

      The rules are simple. Remembering them isn't.

      Most lapses aren't the annual. They're the 24-month items nobody thinks about, and the interval math owners argue about on the forums.

      Calendar months

      Done March 3 or March 31, it's good through March 31.

      "12 calendar months" runs to the end of the month, not the anniversary date. TailBook applies the FAA's reading, leap years included.

      24-month items

      Transponder and pitot-static sneak up on you.

      §91.413 and §91.411 come due every other year, often out of step with the annual. The ELT battery has its own date on the unit.

      Whichever first

      Recurring ADs by hours or calendar.

      "Every 100 hours or 12 months, whichever comes first" needs two clocks. TailBook watches both and projects the hours side from how much you fly.

      FAQ

      Where is my data stored?

      In the app, on your device and in your own private iCloud. There's no TailBook account and no TailBook server holding your logbooks. This free checker runs entirely in your browser; we only receive your email (and the summary shown on your board) if you ask for reminders or join the list.

      Why not just use a spreadsheet?

      You can, and many owners do. Spreadsheets don't apply end-of-month calendar logic, don't handle "hours or months, whichever comes first", don't warn you 30 days out, and don't attach the logbook page that proves it. Co-owners also rarely update the same sheet.

      TailBook is the spreadsheet you'd build if you had a weekend, an IA and a developer, and it lives on your lock screen.

      Does this replace my A&P or IA?

      No. TailBook organizes the dates you enter and tells you what's coming due. It never tells you an aircraft is airworthy. Your A&P/IA performs and signs off the work, and the pilot in command determines airworthiness before each flight. Think of TailBook as the binder that never gets lost and always reads the calendar correctly.

      We're a partnership. How do co-owners work?

      Pricing is per aircraft, not per person. Every co-owner sees the same board through iCloud sharing and can log their own tach or Hobbs time, so cost splits by usage are easy. No logins to manage.

      What does the checker cover?

      Annual (§91.409(a)), 100-hour for aircraft used for hire (§91.409(b), including the 10-hour overfly rule), altimeter/pitot-static for IFR (§91.411), transponder (§91.413), ELT inspection and battery (§91.207), VOR check for IFR (§91.171) and registration (§47.40). The app adds oil changes, recurring ADs and your own custom items.
